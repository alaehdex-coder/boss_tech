06 — How BOSS works

FROM ONE TRANSACTION TO A CLEAR DECISION.

  1. 01

    Record

    Every sale, delivery, purchase and production run is entered once — at the counter, in the warehouse or on the plant floor.

  2. 02

    Connect

    BOSS links each transaction to stock, costs, customers and suppliers automatically — no re-encoding between departments.

  3. 03

    Monitor

    Live dashboards and alerts show what is selling, what is running low, what is delayed and who is overdue.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Reports turn the numbers into answers, so you price, buy, produce and hire on facts instead of guesses.
